Good to know

A few house rules.

  1. Arrive 5 to 10 minutes early and wait outside until your slot.
  2. Sessions run to the minute. Late arrivals do not get extra time.
  3. Booking two slots back to back? You get an extra 5 minutes to change outfits.
  4. The studio is upstairs, with stairs only (not wheelchair accessible).
  5. No alcohol, smoking or pets, sorry. Even very festive ones.

21a/1736 Albany Hwy, Kenwick. Between Ugaily Construction and School of Scape. Upstairs. Free parking out front, overflow at 1731 Albany Hwy.

How many people can be in a session?

Up to 10 people, or 2 families, per 15-minute booking.

Yes. The DIY session is built for phones and cameras. The lighting is already set, so your phone photos look studio-grade.

Yes. 5 extra edits for $50, or 10 for $90, chosen once you have seen your gallery.

Where do I park?

Free parking at the front of the complex at 21a/1736 Albany Hwy, Kenwick. Overflow across the road at 1731 Albany Hwy.
